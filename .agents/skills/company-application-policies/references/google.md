# Google application policy

Treat every Google and Google DeepMind role as manual-only.

## Required actions

- Validate the canonical careers URL, active status, role facts, and fit normally.
- Never open or fill the application form, attach a résumé, claim the job for automation, or assign an application worker.
- Route a qualifying role to `manual_review`, even when its fit score would otherwise queue it.
- Rank Google opportunities against other Google roles found in the same run. Report the title, canonical URL, fit score, concise fit evidence, material gaps, and discovery date.
- Remind the user that their stated constraint is no more than three Google applications in a rolling 30-day window. Do not infer how many slots remain; the ledger does not track applications the user submits manually.
- Ask the user to verify their recent Google application history and choose whether to apply. Do not submit on their behalf.

The purpose of discovery is to preserve good Google opportunities for deliberate selection, not to consume the user's limited allowance automatically.
